Privacy Policy

Kemet Agent is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the YouTube channel "Kemet | Ancient Egypt".

Data it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads data from the owner's own YouTube channel, such as video titles, view counts, comments and analytics. After the owner approves each action, it can update titles, playlists, comment replies and video visibility.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to produce reports and suggestions for the owner and is stored only on the owner's own devices and private accounts. Video titles, view counts and comment text may be sent to Google's Gemini API to generate suggestions; on the free tier, Google may use such data to improve its products. The data is not sold, shared with other third parties, or used for advertising.

Control

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
